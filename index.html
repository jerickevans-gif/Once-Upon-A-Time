Support Our Mission

Once Upon a Time exists to provide children and families with accessible education through music, art, dance, and theater. The mission is to create meaningful experiences that go beyond re-engaging with communities — it’s about fostering growth and building confidence in every child.

What drives us to do the work that we do?

Seeing young performers shine on stage is the heart of this work. Once Upon a Time dedicates every effort to ensuring children receive a high-quality education in the arts, giving them the chance to grow, perform, and make their families proud.

Who benefits?

The entire community benefits — from the children discovering their creative potential to the families who see them grow. This work is made possible by the donors and contributors who invest in our staff and programs, ensuring Once Upon a Time remains a place where the next generation can experience the arts.

How do our programs transform a child’s journey?

Our programs at Once Upon a Time establish the core fundamentals of teamwork. We want to provide children a collaborative space where they can build connections, bring their inner creativity out, and be proud of their accomplishments to share with their parents and families.
