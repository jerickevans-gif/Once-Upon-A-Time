Our Vision & Mission

Once Upon a Time inspires folks to find their creative voice through dance, music, theater, and storytelling. We create spaces for self-expression, connecting families with the community through unforgettable events and performances.

Our mission is to build a vibrant home for artistic and cultural growth — a place where children feel inspired to learn and perform. Supported by dedicated volunteers, instructors, and staff who keep OUAT standing, we work to ensure every child’s talent is seen and valued.

Our Founder Legacy

Founded in 1980 by Aubel and Terry, Once Upon a Time was built on a passion for the arts. Our founders envisioned a world where children could explore art history, theater, and multicultural dance while finding the stage to perform and grow.

Today, OUAT is proudly resurfacing to reconnect with our community through vibrant events and programs. From hosting memorial celebrations and birthdays to building a dedicated home for learners, we invite our neighbors to volunteer and help us keep this creative legacy alive.

Our New Leadership

Once Upon a Time is entering a new chapter under the leadership of Yasmine Rodriguez, who is dedicated to keeping our vision alive for our communities, parents, and alumni. Alongside our core team, we work to ensure every program runs smoothly while giving community contributors a stage to showcase their passions and skills. Together, we remain focused on providing a creative space where every child is inspired to discover their potential and share their talent with the world.
